Fulcra is a hierarchy of accountable AI leads and working sessions, with communication in both directions.

Set the direction.
Let primes coordinate.

Give your primes the outcomes and priorities. A prime coordinates its project leads, connects work across projects and brings the decisions that need you back up.

You → primes → project orchestrators

One prime can lead several projects; several primes can have different remits. You keep the high-level direction instead of managing every working session.

Direction. Decisions. Exceptions.

Your attention belongs on the outcomes and choices that need you. Project leads keep the working detail; primes bring priorities, blockers and decisions back to you.

The streamlined human-attention view is in development. Current project briefs and the decision inbox provide the building blocks.

Implemented in the current sourceRecorded prime/project leadership, project-scoped tasks and sessions, project briefs, decision inbox and configured role-message channels.

In developmentWorkspace umbrellas, retained prime intake and the unified board/communication experience. The board and communication flow is being brought together around each project.
