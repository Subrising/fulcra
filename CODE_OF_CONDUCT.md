# Fulcra community conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained by Subrising/fulcra.

Be respectful and specific. Critique the work rather than the person, assume that people may have different experience, and correct mistakes without harassment. Discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Keep credentials, pairing links, private screenshots and personal machine/session details out of public conversations. Use clean demo material when illustrating a workflow.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and limit participation to keep the space safe and useful. For a conduct concern, ask a repository maintainer for a private reporting route without posting personal details publicly. There is no project-specific reporting email or sponsor channel advertised here. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
